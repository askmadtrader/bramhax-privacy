BramhaX Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

BramhaX is an offline-first Vedic astrology and numerology app. Your privacy matters, and this policy explains how the app handles your information.

Information we collect

BramhaX does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data with us or any third party. Details you enter, such as name, date, time and place of birth, are used only to generate charts and readings on your device.

Data storage

All data stays on your device. Uninstalling the app removes it.

Third parties

The app does not use advertising or analytics services and does not sell or share data.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
